I've written up a short piece for World Mosquito Day (20th August)! Have a read of it on the University of Oxford's news page, [here](https://www.ox.ac.uk/news/2025-08-20-tracking-resistance-mapping-the-spread-of-drug-resistant-malaria).

In other good news, the first part of our geospatial assessment of Japanese encephalitis virus risk in Australia is now in [preprint](https://doi.org/10.31219/osf.io/jhqv6_v1) form! This research is referenced in the [Joint National Japanese Encephalitis Virus Outbreak Response Plan](https://www.cdc.gov.au/resources/publications/joint-national-japanese-encephalitis-virus-outbreak-response-plan), jointly authored by national Departments of Health and Agriculture and distributed by the Australian Centre for Disease Control.
