I presented our current pre-print to colleagues at the Nuffield Department of Medicine Research Symposium last Friday the 4th of September. It was a rare opportunity to see the breadth of disciplines represented across the department, and a fantastic chance to present my research to colleagues outside of my field. I was pleased to take home 3rd prize in the poster competition at the end of the day!

Peer review is progressing on the pre-print and we're hopeful the work will be published soon. Have a look at the pre-print here <a href="https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.03.26347488">here</a>, or go straight to GitHub for the <a href="https://github.com/Infectious-Diseases-Data-Observatory/act_markers_maps">code</a>.
